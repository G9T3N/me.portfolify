# Business Glossary

- **Project:** A showcased item demonstrating work or capabilities.
- **Article:** A blog post or news entry.
- **Experience:** A period of employment, contracting, or internship.
- **Skill:** A specific technology, language, or tool.
- **Availability:** The status of readiness for new work (e.g., available, part-time).
- **Draft/Published/Archived:** Common status lifecycles for content objects.
